I’m an AI and analytics professional who turns messy, high-stakes data—credit risk, fraud, customer behaviour—into models and dashboards that change what a business decides to do next.

MS AI & Business Analytics · University of South Florida

I started in 2019 at Tata Consultancy Services, writing the SQL and building the Power BI and Tableau reports that product and finance teams planned around. At Infosys I moved into banking: tuning a credit-risk scoring model on time-series loan data, building a data-quality framework that cut inconsistencies by 35%, and rewriting the queries behind risk dashboards so they answered 65% faster.

Now I’m looking for a full-time role in AI and analytics—in Tampa, remote, or wherever the right problem is—and I can start immediately.

The AI beat

Where machine learning shows up in my work

“The model is never the deliverable. The decision it changes is.”

I enjoy problems where a better answer changes what someone actually does: which transaction an investigator opens first, which customer gets a retention offer, which region gets more stock. AI is how I get to those answers faster and at scale. Every model I ship comes with an honest account of where it is wrong.

  • GenAI researchAt USF I analysed 1,000+ student interaction records and GenAI activity logs in SQL and Python to measure AI adoption across cohorts and how AI-assisted learning affected performance.
  • Risk modelsAt Infosys I optimised a credit-risk scoring model on time-series loan and transaction data, improving prediction accuracy and reducing default exposure.
  • ClassificationGradient-boosted models for cart abandonment, built in a leak-free pipeline with SMOTE inside cross-validation.
  • ForecastingPer-region Holt-Winters forecasts that are backtested against held-out data before anyone sees them.
  • DetectionFraud-monitoring scenarios with threshold tuning that trades investigator workload against suspicious activity caught.
  • NextLLM-written narrative summaries on top of dashboards, so a chart can explain what changed and why.
